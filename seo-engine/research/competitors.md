# Competitors (web SERP research, 2026-10-28; Semrush unavailable: `no_api_units`)

The real competitors are the brands that show up for our BOFU searches, not only the ones the owner named.

| Competitor | Where it shows up | What it is | Page types seen |
|---|---|---|---|
| Teal | "best AI job search tools", Jobscan alternatives | Job tracker + resume builder | comparisons, reviews by others |
| Huntr | same | Job tracker | own "vs" blog posts (huntr.co/blog/huntr-vs-teal) |
| Jobscan | resume match searches | ATS resume scanner | heavily targeted by others' "alternative" pages |
| Final Round AI | "best AI mock interview" | Interview prep + live copilot | a `/compare` hub of "vs" pages (finalroundai.com/compare) |
| Jobright | resume / job match | AI job matching | reviews of rivals (jobright.ai/blog/jobscan-review-…) |
| Kickresume | "AI tool that finds jobs matching my resume" | Resume builder + AI job matcher | free tool pages |
| Google Interview Warmup | free mock interview | Free practice tool | recommended in many roundups |
| Big Interview, Careery, Simplify, Cake | roundups | various | listicles, "is X worth it" |

## Page types they have that we don't

1. **"Us vs competitor" pages and an "alternatives" hub** (Final Round AI, Huntr, Careery, Jobright all publish them). These are high-converting and heavily cited by AI engines.
2. **Free, no-sign-up tools** (resume-to-job match checkers: BeamJobs, Novoresume, Jobalytics). This is the most common way rivals win "resume match score" searches.
3. **"Is X worth it" / review pages** written about competitors.

## Gaps nobody covers well

- **Dice in the same search as LinkedIn and Indeed.** The multi-board tools found (Apify scraper, JobHunt AI, JobGrid) list LinkedIn/Indeed/Glassdoor/ZipRecruiter but do not confirm Dice. JobHunter's Dice coverage is a real differentiator for tech job seekers.
- **One tool for the whole loop** (find → match → email → voice interview). Roundups recommend combining several tools by stage, and JobHunter covers all four stages.
- **"Never applies for you."** Roundups warn that auto-apply tools can get accounts flagged. JobHunter's no-auto-apply stance answers that fear directly.
