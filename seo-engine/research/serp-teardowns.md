# SERP teardowns (web search, US, 2026-10-28)

Volume and difficulty are unavailable (Semrush `no_api_units`), so this is qualitative only.

| Target | What ranks | Format to match | Our angle (information gain) |
|---|---|---|---|
| best ai job search tools | Vendor/affiliate listicles (hackajob, careery, jobr, dev.to, skillscouter) | Dated comparison listicle with a real table | Ours already exists, is dated and sourced. Add a "by job-search stage" table and an honest auto-apply warning section |
| best ai mock interview tool | Roundups (fast.io, codersera, favtutor, prachub) | Listicle; practice vs live-copilot split | Voice-first practice with follow-ups and a 0-10 score. Strictly practice, not a live copilot |
| free ai mock interview with voice | Small vendor product pages + Product Hunt | Product page with free-tier terms stated up front | /features/ai-mock-interview should state "1 mock interview a month on the Free plan" near the top (CONFIRMED limit) |
| resume job description match score | Free tool pages (BeamJobs, Novoresume, Jobalytics) | Interactive free tool | We have no on-site tool; the feature page must make the free match score (2/month on Free) obvious |
| AI tool that finds jobs matching my resume | Kickresume, Resumatch, extensions | Product page | Three boards in one search, ranked, with missing skills |
| search LinkedIn Indeed Dice at once | Scrapers, extensions, app lists | Product page / how-to | Strongest gap: Dice coverage is rare |
| jobscan alternative / final round ai alternatives | Aggregators (alternativeto, topai.tools) + vendor roundups | "Alternatives" listicle with a comparison table | Honest, dated, sourced, with JobHunter disclosed as ours |
| how to answer tell me about yourself | Large career sites (cake.me, tallo, collegetransitions, insightglobal) | Long guide with examples by situation | Examples for switchers, returners and new grads, plus "practice it out loud" with the HR & Introduction round |

SERP features seen: AI Overviews and People Also Ask on how-to queries. Listicles and aggregators dominate "best" and "alternatives" queries.
