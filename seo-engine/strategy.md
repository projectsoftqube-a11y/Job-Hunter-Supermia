# Strategy (Checkpoint 1)

## What this means for you

- Google can read every page properly now, and the technical errors in Search Console are fixed in code.
- Nobody (Google or AI assistants like ChatGPT) mentions JobHunter yet. That's normal for a site that is a few weeks old.
- The searches you can win first are the ones where JobHunter is genuinely different: **one search across LinkedIn, Indeed *and* Dice**, and **voice mock interviews with a score**.
- Big "best AI job search tool" lists are owned by other websites. You get into those mainly by being *listed on them* (outreach), not by out-writing them.
- Next pages to add: a third interview-prep article (which also unlocks a hidden category page), an About page, and two honest "alternatives" pages.
- Nothing here guarantees a #1 ranking. Links and mentions from other sites, plus time, do the rest.

## Buyers and their words

See `fact-sheet.md` → buyer profiles. Their words: "AI job search", "jobs that match my resume", "practice interview out loud", "mock interview with feedback", "compare resume to job description", "Jobscan alternative", "tell me about yourself".

## Topic areas we can win (and why)

1. **Multi-board job search incl. Dice.** No mainstream tool in the results confirms Dice. Strongest for software engineers.
2. **Voice AI mock interview practice** with follow-ups, 4 rounds and a 0-10 score. Small vendors rank, so the field is winnable.
3. **Resume-to-job match with missing skills.** Free tools dominate; we compete on "match per job + what to fix + then practice".
4. **Interview answers by situation** (switchers, returners, new grads). Big sites rank for the generic query; our audience pages give us situational depth.
5. **Honest alternatives/comparisons**, disclosed as ours, dated and sourced.

## Where competitors beat us

They publish "vs" and "alternatives" pages, offer free no-sign-up tools, and appear on listicles and directories (Product Hunt, AlternativeTo, G2). Our gap is off-site presence more than on-page work.

## What AI engines say about us

Nothing yet (see `research/ai-visibility.md`). The brand name "Job Hunter" is shared with several other tools, so always say "JobHunter by SuperMIA" off-site.
